03 Approach

Ship it. Then
make it good.

I like the loop where a rough idea runs end to end on day one and every pass after that is about removing friction — fewer clicks, faster loads, clearer words.

  1. 01

    Working beats perfect

    A version you can click teaches you more than a spec you can argue about.

  2. 02

    Defaults are design

    The first thing a person sees — and the first thing they can undo — sets the tone.

  3. 03

    Keep it legible

    Code, copy and UI all get read by someone else. Optimise for that someone.
