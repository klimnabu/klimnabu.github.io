01 The story

Two outlaws.
One state of mind.

Lucia and Jason are in love — and in trouble. Somewhere between the neon of Vice City and the backroads of Leonida, they discover that the only way out is to make one more score.

Grand Theft Auto VI takes the series to the state of Leonida, home to the neon-soaked streets of Vice City and beyond. The biggest, most immersive evolution of the series yet — built on the next generation of the RAGE engine.
